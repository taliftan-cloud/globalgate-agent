# NNN Agreements (Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention)

## What an NNN agreement covers

A standard Western-style Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is often unenforceable against a
Chinese manufacturer, because Chinese courts generally will not enforce a foreign-law contract
against a domestic factory, and a pure NDA does not address the two failure modes that matter
most when sourcing from a Chinese supplier:

- **Non-Use**: the supplier is prohibited from using the buyer's design, drawings, or
  specifications for any purpose other than fulfilling the buyer's own purchase orders —
  including manufacturing a near-identical product for a third party.
- **Non-Circumvention**: the supplier is prohibited from approaching the buyer's own customers,
  distributors, or end markets directly, cutting the buyer out of the relationship it originated.

An NNN agreement is therefore drafted specifically to be enforceable **in China**: it should be
governed by Chinese law, name a competent Chinese court (typically in the jurisdiction where the
supplier's factory or registered address is located) or a recognized Chinese arbitration body,
and be executed in Chinese (a bilingual English-Chinese version with the Chinese text controlling
in the event of conflict is standard practice).

## Key clauses to include

1. **Precise definition of Confidential Information** — technical drawings, tooling
   specifications, bill of materials, and any patented or patent-pending design elements.
2. **Liquidated damages clause** — a specific, pre-agreed damages figure (rather than "actual
   damages") significantly increases the odds of a Chinese court actually enforcing the
   agreement, since proving actual damages from IP misuse is notoriously difficult.
3. **Factory-level scope** — the agreement should bind the specific legal entity operating the
   factory, not just a trading company or sourcing agent that may have no manufacturing capacity
   of its own and limited ability to actually prevent misuse.
4. **Term and survival** — non-use and non-circumvention obligations should survive termination
   of the underlying purchase relationship for a meaningful period (commonly 3-5 years).

## When it matters most

NNN protection is most critical when a product is based on the buyer's own proprietary design or
patent, rather than an off-the-shelf item the factory already manufactures for many customers —
in the latter case there is little proprietary information to protect in the first place.

*This document is a general informational overview, not legal advice. Always have an NNN
agreement reviewed by a licensed attorney qualified in the relevant Chinese jurisdiction before
relying on it.*
